## 7. Awards Workstream

### Best Paper Awards Committee

The best paper awards process is being handled through a small, conflict-free committee. The process is intended to keep substantive award evaluation separate from general conference logistics.

Current working principles:

- Avoid committee members who are authors, co-authors, presenters, advisor/student mentors, close collaborators, or current professional partners of likely finalist papers.
- Provide committee members with finalist papers, reviewer-score context, evaluation criteria, and conflict-of-interest instructions.
- Treat CFP Board / FPLS award papers separately from AFS best paper awards because they are being featured in the dedicated CFP Board FPLS Best Paper Award Session.
- Confirm current paid-up AFS membership before award recipients are finalized.

### Early Career Award

The call for nominations has been circulated. Nominations are due by **September 15**. This award is being tracked separately from the AFS best paper awards because it is based on career-stage eligibility, publication record, and service/volunteer activity rather than conference-paper quality alone.

### Stuart Michelson Memorial Award

The Stuart Michelson Memorial Award is being coordinated with FSR leadership. This award is separate from the AFS conference-paper awards because it is based on the best *Financial Services Review* paper from the relevant prior award period.

### Dalton Best Poster Award

The Dalton Best Poster Award is tied to the poster session and should follow the poster voting process. Shannon / Dalton may MC the poster session and announce the winner, subject to final logistics.
